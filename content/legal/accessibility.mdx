We want this site to work for the people it is for, including people using a
screen reader, a keyboard alone, magnification, or a phone on a poor connection.
This page says what we have built, what we have not finished, and how to tell us
when we have got it wrong.

## What is built in

Every page can be operated with a keyboard. Whatever you are on shows a visible
focus outline, and the first thing you reach with the tab key is a link that
skips the navigation and takes you straight to the main content.

If your device is set to reduce motion, the site honours it. Animation drops to
almost nothing and the page stops scrolling smoothly, because that setting is
usually there for a reason.

The site reads in a light or a dark theme, and your choice is remembered. Colour
pairings are chosen against a minimum contrast of 4.5 to 1 for body text.

The content of every page can be read with JavaScript turned off.

Forms label every field, mark the ones that are required, and put any error next
to the field it belongs to rather than only at the top.

## What is not finished

No independent accessibility audit has been carried out yet, so we are not
claiming a WCAG conformance level. Saying we conform when nobody has checked
would be worth nothing to you.

Automated checks run against our component library on every build, in both
themes, using the WCAG 2.2 A and AA rule sets. Automated checks catch a minority
of real problems. They are not a substitute for someone testing the site with
the assistive technology they actually use, and that testing has not happened
yet.

Some pages are still being built and are deliberately thin.

## Telling us it does not work

If any part of this site is difficult or impossible for you to use, please tell
us through the [contact page](/contact). Describe what you were trying to do and
what happened. You do not need to know the technical reason, and you do not need
to use any particular words.

We will reply, and we will tell you honestly whether we can fix it and roughly
when. If something is blocking you today, say so and we will find another way to
get you what you need in the meantime.
