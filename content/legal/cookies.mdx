## The short version

This site sets no cookies. There is no advertising on it, no analytics, and
nothing that follows you anywhere else. That is also why you have not been asked
to accept anything: there is nothing to ask you about.

## What is kept on your device

Two things. Both are stored by your own browser, and neither is sent to us.

Your theme choice is saved as `theme` in local storage. It is written when you
use the light and dark toggle, and it is what keeps the site in the mode you
picked the next time you come back.

Whether the opening animation has already run is saved as `oth:intro-drawn` in
session storage. It stops the line redrawing every time you move between pages.
Session storage is emptied by your browser when you close the tab.

Neither one holds your name, your email address, or anything else about you.
Neither can be read by another website.

## How to clear them

Clear site data for this domain in your browser settings, usually under privacy
or site permissions. Both are then gone. Nothing breaks: the site returns to the
light theme, and the opening animation plays once more.

## What your browser loads

Pages, stylesheets and typefaces all come from this site. The fonts are served
from our own server rather than from a font network, so opening a page here does
not tell a third party that you visited.

## Forms

If you send a booking, an enquiry or a referral, what you type goes to our own
server. That is personal information rather than browser storage, so it is
covered by the [privacy notice](/legal/privacy) and not by this page.

## If this changes

If we ever add something that stores more than the two items above, this page
changes before it ships.
