## 1. Brand foundation

### Positioning statement

For prison leavers who want real work, real qualifications, and a real chance, **Off the Hook** is a hospitality-led social enterprise where world-class food is cooked, served, and taught by people rebuilding their lives. Unlike traditional charities, we operate as a working restaurant and academy, so training is paid, live, and accredited — not simulated.

### The tagline

**Real work. Real qualifications. Real chances.**

The three "reals" carry the whole brand. Any communication that dilutes them — with softer language, hedging, or corporate uplift words like "empowering" or "transformative" — is off-brand.

### Brand promises

Three promises the brand must keep in everything it does:

1. **Honest, not sentimental.** We do not trade on pity. We show the work.
2. **Warm, not corporate.** Every touchpoint feels made by hand, not by committee.
3. **Serious about craft.** Food, service, and design all held to a professional standard.

### Personality

Five adjectives that guide every decision. If a piece of work doesn't feel like these, it isn't Off the Hook.

- **Grounded.** No spin. Named people, real numbers, plain speech.
- **Warm.** A little humour. Never cold or clinical.
- **Considered.** Editorial rhythm, thought given to the smallest detail.
- **Direct.** Short sentences win. No corporate uplift words.
- **Optimistic without naivety.** We are honest about what a criminal record costs, and we believe the work is worth doing.

## 2. Audience mental model

Every piece of work should be able to answer: which of these people is this for?

- **Diner** — coming for a good meal. Wants to know it will be delicious and welcoming, learns the mission at the door and stays for the story.
- **Referral partner (probation, prison, third sector)** — needs to trust the process quickly. Wants clarity on safeguarding, outcomes, and how referrals work.
- **Funder or commissioner** — wants outcomes, measurement, and credibility.
- **Trainee** — wants to feel welcome, not judged. Wants the practical facts.
- **Employer** — wants to know the calibre of the qualifications and the person.

Doc 1 (Master PRD) has the full personas. This document uses them as a filter.

## 3. Brand architecture

Off the Hook operates as a CIC with two publicly named programmes:

- **Off the Hook Restaurant** — the customer-facing hospitality experience.
- **Off the Hook Academy** — the training programme.

Both sit under one master brand. There is no separate identity for either. In use they appear as **"The restaurant"** and **"The academy"** in navigation, and as **"Off the Hook Restaurant"** and **"Off the Hook Academy"** in formal contexts (business cards, funder documents, press releases).

CIC number, registered address, and safeguarding contact appear in the footer of every document and page. This is legally required and also reinforces credibility.

## 4. Logo

### 4.1 Direction

The name "Off the Hook" carries two meanings we want to earn: (1) *off the hook* as in released, free, given a chance, and (2) *off the hook* as in food so good it flies off the pass. We lean gently on both without labouring either.

Three logo directions to explore. The direction sketches accompany this document as separate images. Choose one before commissioning final artwork.

**Direction A — Editorial wordmark.**
"Off the Hook" set in Fraunces Variable at weight 500 with SOFT axis at 100 and slight WONK, in `--ink-900` on `--cream-50`. All lowercase. Slightly tightened tracking. Beneath it, "CIC" set in Inter Variable, uppercase, letter-spaced +80, weight 500, in `--forest-600`. This is the safest, most editorial route. Reads like a good restaurant sign or a small independent magazine.

**Direction B — Monogram + wordmark.**
A small monogram sits to the left of the wordmark: a single lowercase "o" in Fraunces with a subtle hook-shaped counter (the negative space in the letter) drawn slightly asymmetric so it reads as a hook without being literal. The wordmark sits to its right, same specification as Direction A. On mobile favicons and app icons, only the monogram appears.

**Direction C — Hook mark + wordmark.**
A minimalist hook — a single curved stroke, 1.5px on a 24px grid — sits to the left of the wordmark. The mark is drawn with the same visual weight as the letter strokes, so it reads as part of the type family rather than an added logo mark. Warmer and slightly more literal than B.

The pack recommends **Direction A or B**. Direction C risks being too literal and dating quickly.

### 4.2 Construction

Whichever direction is chosen, these rules apply.

**Grid.** The wordmark sits on a 24-unit vertical grid where 1 unit equals the cap height ÷ 4. The x-height sits at 12 units, the cap height at 16 units, the ascender at 20 units, the descender at -4 units.

**Clear space.** The clear space around the logo on all sides equals the height of the letter "H" in the wordmark. Nothing else — no other logo, no image edge, no text, no interactive control — may enter that space.

**Minimum size.** Wordmark: 96px wide on screen, 24mm wide in print. Monogram alone: 24px on screen, 8mm in print. Below these sizes, use the monogram alone or the CIC number in Inter without the mark.

### 4.3 Colour usage

The logo is used only in these three colour combinations:

| Combination | Wordmark | "CIC" | Background |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary (light) | `--ink-900` | `--forest-600` | `--cream-50` |
| Reverse (dark) | `--cream-25` | `--forest-500-dark` | `--night-950` |
| Monochrome (print, embroidery) | `--ink-900` | `--ink-900` | any solid light background |

The logo is never used in `--forest-600` on `--cream-50` as the primary combination. Forest is the accent, not the wordmark colour. Ink stays ink.

### 4.4 Misuse

The logo is never:

- Stretched, condensed, or distorted.
- Rotated at any angle.
- Placed inside a container, box, or badge unless it is the reverse variant used on a dark photo.
- Given a drop shadow, glow, outline, or bevel.
- Rendered in a colour outside the three combinations above.
- Placed on a busy photograph where the background competes with the letterforms. If in doubt, put a solid card behind it.
- Cropped, truncated, or partially obscured.
- Reproduced with a texture, gradient, or fill pattern.
- Recreated in a different typeface. Never.

If a partner asks for the logo, send the SVG. Do not let anyone re-typeset it.

### 5.2 Usage ratios

A rough guide to how much of each colour appears on any given surface.

- **60% cream family** (background, cards, dividers)
- **30% ink family** (text, primary content)
- **8% forest** (accent, links, primary CTAs)
- **2% secondary accents** (olive, copper — used sparingly for categorisation)

Reversed for dark theme: 60% night, 30% cream (text), 8% forest, 2% secondary.

Anything that violates these ratios reads as off-brand. If the site starts to look "too green", forest has been overused. If it starts to look "too brown", cream has been underused.

### 5.3 Contrast (WCAG AA)

Every text/background pair must pass WCAG AA (4.5:1 for body, 3:1 for large text).

| Text | Background | Ratio | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| ink-900 | cream-50 | 13.8:1 | Pass AAA |
| ink-700 | cream-50 | 10.4:1 | Pass AAA |
| ink-500 | cream-50 | 5.9:1 | Pass AA (body), fail AAA (body) |
| forest-600 | cream-50 | 6.9:1 | Pass AA (large), pass AA (body) |
| cream-50 | forest-600 | 6.9:1 | Pass AA — reverse |
| cream-25 | night-950 | 14.2:1 | Pass AAA |
| cream-200 | night-950 | 10.1:1 | Pass AAA |
| forest-500 | night-950 | 4.9:1 | Pass AA (body) |
| copper-500 | cream-50 | 4.6:1 | Pass AA (body, just) — use for tags only |
| olive-500 | cream-50 | 3.9:1 | Fail AA body, pass large text only |

**Rule:** olive-500 never appears as body text on cream. It is a tag colour only.

### 5.4 Colour meanings

Colours carry meaning across the site. Do not repurpose them for decoration.

- **Forest.** Primary action. Anything the user should click first.
- **Copper.** Qualifications and accreditations. Never for calls to action.
- **Olive.** Education and academy content. A quieter accent than forest.
- **Ink browns.** Text and identity. Never used as a button fill.
- **Cream.** Paper. It's the surface everything else sits on.
- **Status danger.** Errors and destructive actions only. Never decorative.

### 5.5 Gradients

There are no gradient fills anywhere in the brand. Solid colour only. This is a deliberate choice against the fashion for gradient-heavy CIC branding, which dates quickly and reads as generic.

## 6. Typography

### 6.1 Type stack

Two families, both variable, both self-hosted via Fontsource.

- **Display: Fraunces Variable.** OFL licence. Axes used: `wght` 300-700, `SOFT` 0-100, `WONK` 0-1.
- **Text: Inter Variable.** OFL licence. Axes used: `wght` 300-700.

Fallbacks are declared in Doc 7 section 3 and must be respected.

### 6.2 Scale

Fluid, one line covers phones through 4K. Values are `clamp(min, preferred, max)`.

| Token | Size range | Family | Weight | Line height | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--fs-display` | 44px → 84px | Fraunces | 500 (SOFT 30) | 1.12 | Home hero, section heros |
| `--fs-h1` | 34px → 56px | Fraunces | 500 | 1.15 | Page titles |
| `--fs-h2` | 28px → 40px | Fraunces | 500 | 1.2 | Section titles |
| `--fs-h3` | 22px → 26px | Fraunces | 500 | 1.25 | Sub-sections, card titles |
| `--fs-h4` | 18px → 20px | Inter | 600 | 1.3 | Small labels, form group headings |
| `--fs-lead` | 18px → 20px | Inter | 400 | 1.5 | Intro paragraphs |
| `--fs-body` | 16px → 17px | Inter | 400 | 1.6 | Body copy |
| `--fs-small` | 14px | Inter | 400 | 1.5 | Meta, captions |
| `--fs-tiny` | 12px | Inter | 500 | 1.4 | Eyebrows (uppercase, +80 tracking), legal footer |

### 6.3 Typographic rules

- **Measure.** Body copy caps at `68ch` (roughly 62-72 characters per line). Any line longer than that is hard to read.
- **Alignment.** Left align. No justification anywhere. No centre-aligned paragraphs.
- **Widows and orphans.** Manually break long headings with a soft break so the last line is not one word. Use `<br class="hidden md:inline" />` or `text-wrap: balance` in browsers that support it.
- **Sentence case.** Headings and buttons are sentence case, not title case. Exception: proper nouns, brand names.
- **Numerals.** Metrics use `tabular-nums` so digits align in count-ups. Body copy uses default (proportional) numerals.
- **Ligatures on** for Fraunces (common ligatures like `st`, `ffi`). Never discretionary ligatures — they look showy.

### 6.4 Pull quotes

Set in Fraunces at `--fs-h3` in `--ink-900`, italic axis on, with 32px hanging indent on the opening quote mark. Attribution below in Inter at `--fs-small`, `--ink-500`.

Example:

```
"I never had a job I was proud of. Now I make bread every morning
 and someone eats it. That's it. That's the difference."
                                                    — Danny, trainee, 2026
```

### 6.5 Eyebrows

Small labels above section titles. Inter, `--fs-tiny`, weight 500, uppercase, letter-spacing +80, `--forest-600`. Two to five words maximum. They set the tone of the section that follows.

Example: `THE RESTAURANT` above a headline like "A room that changes lives".

## 7. Motion in the brand

The two curves (`--ease-enter` and `--ease-exit`) are as much a brand asset as the colour palette. Every animation on any surface — website, social video, presentation — uses those two curves.

Full motion system is in Doc 5. The brand-book summary is:

- Motion is meaningful, short, and restrained.
- No bouncing, no elastic overshoot, no fly-in effects.
- Reduced motion is a first-class alternative.

## 8. Iconography

### 8.1 Library

**Lucide** as the primary icon library. All icons are open-source, MIT licensed, and drawn on a consistent 24x24 grid with 1.5px strokes and rounded joins.

Icon sizes on screen:

- 16px — inline in body text
- 20px — form fields, list items
- 24px — buttons, nav
- 32px — feature cards
- 48px — large feature blocks (rare)

### 8.2 Custom icons for the journey

The seven-step journey uses seven custom icons, drawn to match Lucide's stroke weight and grid. Each icon is a small metaphor:

| Step | Icon direction |
|---|---|
| 1. Prison | A closed door with a small key hole, key not present |
| 2. Referral | Two hands, one passing a folded letter to the other |
| 3. Induction | A cook's apron, tied |
| 4. Training | A whisk, a knife, and a wooden spoon crossed |
| 5. Service | A plate with a rim mark, held level |
| 6. Qualification | A certificate seal (simple concentric circles + ribbon) |
| 7. Employment | An open door with a light on beyond it |

Each icon is 32x32 on a 24x24 grid, 1.5px stroke, `--ink-900` at rest, `--forest-600` when active. See Doc 5 section J1 for the timeline animation and section 5 for the once-per-icon draw animation.

### 8.3 Rules

- No filled icons. Outline only, consistent with Lucide.
- No icons inside form field labels.
- Icons paired with text always sit **left** of the text, 8px gap.
- Never use icons as a decorative flourish. Every icon means something.

## 9. Illustration

Off the Hook uses **photography, not illustration**. The one exception is the custom journey icons above.

If a section absolutely cannot be photographed (a data story, a legal explainer), use a small typographic diagram or a plain quote — never a stock illustration in flat vector style. Stock illustration is banned outright. It ages badly and undermines the honest tone of the brand.

## 10. Photography direction

Photography is the single most important brand asset after the logo and the palette. Get it right and everything else works.

### 10.1 Overall direction

**One line:** shot on the day, not styled for a shoot. Natural light. Real hands. Real kitchens. Real service.

Reference set (do not copy): Rapha Journal, MR PORTER Journal, The Guardian Feast, Toklas London's Instagram, St. JOHN's press stills, Wildfarmed's brand imagery, Migrateful's website, Cereal Magazine.

### 10.2 The don'ts (hard rules)

- **No prison imagery.** No bars, no locks, no orange jumpsuits, no shots taken inside a prison.
- **No handcuffs**, no chains, no anything that reduces a person to their record.
- **No before/after face splits.** Not "look how far he's come".
- **No stock photography.** Ever. If real photography is not ready, the site uses a solid `--cream-100` placeholder with an eyebrow label, not a stock image.
- **No fine-dining tweezer plating** photography. This is honest food, not tweezer food.
- **No wide over-lit corporate shots** of people in blazers shaking hands.
- **No cropped hero shots of the founder** in a suit against a solid background.
- **No black-and-white** as a stylistic default. Only if a specific story needs it.
- **No filters that flatten skin tones** (Instagram-style desaturation on brown or black skin is off-brand and offensive).

### 10.3 The do's

- **Warm, natural white balance.** Not cold, not orange. Slightly warm-neutral, ~5400K.
- **Available light where possible.** Kitchen light, window light, service light. No hard flashes.
- **Grain over sharpening.** A little grain reads as film and forgives skin. Sharpening reads as corporate.
- **Faces at their own eye level** when in frame. Not from above, not from below.
- **Hands, always hands.** Cooking hands, plating hands, welding hands, writing hands, holding hands.
- **The room, not just the food.** Wide shots of the pass, the dining room, the workshop are as important as tight food shots.
- **Movement allowed.** A slightly blurred hand pouring a sauce is more honest than a frozen hero shot.
- **Portraits with permission.** Anyone photographed on the record has signed the photography consent form (see the safeguarding policy).

### 10.5 Photographer brief

If commissioning a photographer, brief them with this document. Non-negotiables in the brief:

- Read this document before the shoot.
- Bring one camera, one prime lens, no ring lights.
- Shoot at ISO 400-1600 for grain.
- No post-production skin smoothing. None.
- Deliver RAW and JPG.
- Grant unlimited-use rights to Off the Hook CIC for both stills and derivatives.

Photographer day rate should be budgeted at £600-800 for a full day in the UK independent sector. Do not accept "free" shoots in exchange for credit. It never delivers.

### 10.6 File formats

- **Web:** `.webp` at three sizes (480w, 960w, 1920w). Storage bucket public-media resizes on upload (see Doc 6 section 4.1).
- **Print:** `.tiff` for print, minimum 300dpi at final size.
- **Social:** `.jpg` at 90% quality, sized per platform (see section 13.4).
- **Alt text:** every image, no exceptions. Descriptive, not decorative.

## 11. Voice and tone

The full voice rules live in the `human-voice-writer` skill. The brand-specific edits are here.

### 11.1 Voice principles

1. **Named, not "our team".** People have names. Anne, Danny, Sarah. Never "our valued trainees".
2. **Number, not adjective.** "Forty-two trainees this year" not "many trainees".
3. **Verb, not verb-noun.** "We cook" not "we deliver culinary services".
4. **Full stops win.** Short sentences. When in doubt, two short sentences beat one long one.

### 11.2 Banned words in Off the Hook copy

In addition to the general skill's banned list, these words specifically do not appear in Off the Hook communications:

- **Empowering.** People are already whole. We offer work.
- **Transformative.** Same problem. If the work works, say so plainly.
- **Beneficiaries.** They are trainees, alumni, staff, or diners. Never beneficiaries.
- **Journey** (as a metaphor for personal change). We use "journey" only for the seven-step programme where it means a literal path.
- **Ex-offenders.** We say "prison leavers" or the person's name. "Ex-offender" reduces a person to a legal category.
- **Vulnerable.** Descriptive when accurate (e.g. in safeguarding policy), banned as a general adjective for our people.
- **Give back.** We do not need diners to give back. We need them to eat and enjoy.

### 11.3 Tone dial

The same brand can speak in different registers depending on the audience. Use the dial.

| Audience | Register |
|---|---|
| Diner | Warm, editorial, food-first |
| Referral partner | Direct, practical, safeguarding-first |
| Funder | Confident, evidence-first, no sentiment |
| Trainee | Direct, welcoming, no jargon, no charity language |
| Press | Confident, quotable, named |
| Alumni | Family register — warm but not sentimental |

### 11.4 Examples

**Off-brand:**
> Off the Hook empowers vulnerable ex-offenders on a transformative journey back into society through the power of hospitality.

**On-brand:**
> We are a working restaurant in [city]. Our kitchen team is half professional chefs, half trainees leaving prison. If the food is good, we all get paid, and forty-two people so far have left with a job and a City & Guilds.

**Off-brand:**
> Come dine at Off the Hook and give back today!

**On-brand:**
> Book a table. Eat well. Everyone who works here earns a wage.

**Off-brand (referral partner):**
> Partner with us to unlock impact for your service users.

**On-brand (referral partner):**
> Refer someone with 6-12 weeks left on their sentence. We meet them in the last month, take them through induction, and start them the week after release. Safeguarding lead is Anne Kiragu.

### 11.5 Rhythm rule

Read every paragraph aloud in your head before publishing. If two consecutive sentences are the same length, break one. If a sentence needs a comma, ask whether it needs two shorter sentences instead.

## 12. Application — digital

### 12.1 Website

Fully specified across Docs 2-7 and 9. Highlights:

- Two themes: Cream light (default), Deep brown dark.
- The site is fully responsive from 320px to 1920px.
- The site never uses more than 5 colours on a single page (background, text, one accent, one secondary, and status).
- The site never uses more than 4 icons in one section.

### 12.2 Email templates

Three template shapes. Full HTML in the build; the brand-book summary is:

- **Newsletter.** Cream background, single column, 600px max width. Fraunces H2 for section titles, Inter for body. One primary CTA in `--forest-600`. Footer in `--ink-500` at `--fs-small` with the CIC number, address, and unsubscribe link.
- **Transactional (booking confirmation, referral acknowledgement).** Same shape as newsletter but with a system tone. Subject lines are direct: "Your booking at Off the Hook — Sat 20 Sep, 7:30pm". No exclamation marks.
- **Fundraising ask.** Same shape. Never uses charity guilt language. Frames the ask as "if you liked what happened here, help another cohort start in October".

### 12.3 Presentations

For funder decks and press packs, use Google Slides or Keynote templates with:

- 16:9 aspect ratio.
- Cream background, ink text, forest accents.
- Fraunces for slide titles, Inter for body.
- One idea per slide. No bullet lists longer than 4 items.
- Photographs bleed to the edge, no shadow, no border.

## 13. Application — social

### 13.1 Platforms

- **Instagram.** Primary platform. Posts, stories, reels.
- **LinkedIn.** Secondary. Longer-form posts from Anne. Funder-facing.
- **Facebook.** Tertiary. Event listings and community updates.
- **TikTok.** Not launched at v1. Consider phase 2.
- **X/Twitter.** Not launched. Off-brand risk.

### 13.2 Instagram grid

- **1080 x 1350** portrait posts by default (Instagram's preferred grid ratio).
- **1080 x 1080** square posts for logo-only announcements.
- Grid reads editorially: three photographs, one typographic post (a quote or a metric), repeat. Every three rows should have at least one typographic post.

### 13.3 Instagram templates

Three post templates:

- **Photo post.** Full-bleed photograph. Caption in the Instagram body, not on the image. Never text over a face.
- **Metric post.** Cream background, Fraunces display number in `--ink-900`, one-line label in Inter `--fs-body` in `--ink-700`. Small tagline in the bottom-left corner in `--forest-600`.
- **Quote post.** Cream background, Fraunces italic pull quote centred, attribution below in Inter. Never mixed with photography.

### 13.4 Social image sizes

| Platform | Format | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram feed portrait | JPG | 1080 x 1350 |
| Instagram feed square | JPG | 1080 x 1080 |
| Instagram story | JPG or MP4 | 1080 x 1920 |
| Instagram reel cover | JPG | 1080 x 1920 |

| LinkedIn document post | PDF | 1200 x 1500 per page |

### 13.5 Social voice

Same voice as the website. Instagram captions are two to five sentences maximum. Story text is even shorter. Reels always have captions burnt into the video for accessibility.

Never post about a trainee without written consent for that specific post.

Never post a "throwback" of a former trainee without checking with them first.

## 14. Application — menu, signage, uniform, print

### 14.1 Menu card

- **Paper.** Uncoated warm-white or manila 120gsm, printed one side.
- **Size.** A5 portrait.
- **Type.** Section titles in Fraunces `--fs-h3` in `--ink-900`. Items in Inter weight 500. Prices tabular-nums right-aligned. Descriptions in Inter weight 400 in `--ink-700`.
- **Header.** The wordmark at the top, centred, 60mm wide.
- **Footer.** Two lines. Line 1: allergen note. Line 2: "All wages paid. CIC no. [pending]."
- **Refresh.** Reprinted weekly with the changing specials. Handwritten specials on a chalkboard behind the pass.

### 14.2 Signage

- **Exterior sign.** The wordmark on a `--cream-50` painted panel with `--forest-600` "CIC" and `--ink-900` letterforms. Backlit warm white, not bright. Cast metal or hand-painted vinyl.
- **Interior.** Room signage in the same Fraunces/Inter combination as digital. Table numbers hand-painted on wood or ceramic, no plastic.
- **Way-finding.** For the academy space, small brass plates engraved with room names ("Kitchen 1", "Kitchen 2", "Classroom", "Locker room").

### 14.3 Uniform

## 17. Misuse and don'ts

A summary of the hard rules, gathered so a partner or supplier can read one page before working with the brand.

**Logo:**
- Do not re-typeset the wordmark.
- Do not add effects.
- Do not put the logo in a colour combination other than the three approved combinations.
- Do not squash, rotate, or crop.

**Colour:**
- Do not use gradients.
- Do not use pure white.
- Do not use red or orange except for `--status-danger` in destructive UI.
- Do not use `--forest-600` as body text.
- Do not use `--olive-500` as body text.

**Type:**
- Do not use a typeface other than Fraunces and Inter.
- Do not centre body paragraphs.
- Do not justify text.
- Do not use SHOUTING CAPS in body copy.
- Do not use italics as emphasis in Inter (use weight 500 instead).

**Photography:**
- Do not use stock photography.
- Do not photograph handcuffs, bars, or prison interiors.
- Do not do before/after portraits.
- Do not tweezer-plate food.
- Do not photograph people without written consent.

**Voice:**
- Do not use "empowering", "transformative", "beneficiaries", "vulnerable" (general), "ex-offenders", "give back".
- Do not centre a message on pity.
- Do not exclaim.

**Motion:**
- Do not bounce.
- Do not fly in from any direction other than a small vertical rise.
- Do not autoplay video with sound.
