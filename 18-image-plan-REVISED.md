## What changed and why

v1 locked the look to a Michelin bar: Core by Clare Smyth, The Ledbury, Le Manoir, crisp white linen, refined plating with negative space on fine porcelain. It explicitly rejected the honest register as "the miss."

That was wrong for this business. Anne worked the level herself and landed between fine dining and home cooking. The reference she brought is live-fire cooking: spiced meat over open flame, heavy char, real smoke. That is the register, and it is now locked.

This also settles a conflict that already existed in the pack. Docs 15 and 16 (photography direction and shoot brief) were always warm, honest and documentary, naming St. John and Blacklock as the bar. v1 pushed against them. v2 brings the image plan back into line with 15 and 16 rather than fighting them.

## The bar, locked

Live-fire, spice-led, elevated casual. Serious about the cooking, relaxed about everything else.

Reference bar: Berber & Q, Black Axe Mangal, Kiln, Smoking Goat, Chishuru, Brat. Warm, confident, generous. Open flame and hard char are the point, not something to hide.

Read on the food: bold spice, deep colour, real char on the edges, generous portions, a little mess allowed. Plated on warm hand-thrown or reactive-glaze ceramic, or on a board, never fine white porcelain. No tweezers, no foam, no negative-space plating, no smears.

Read on the room: warmth and character. Wood, warm plaster or brick, simple glassware, the grill visible or implied. Lived-in, not precious.

What this is not: not white tablecloth, not tweezer food, not a takeaway flyer, not a rustic comfort-food bistro. The distance from a flyer is held in the grade and the framing, not by making the food posher.

## The grade, and the one line that keeps this from tipping

Grade holds from Docs 15 and 16, unchanged: one believable source, soft directional daylight, roughly 3800 to 4600K, warm espresso shadows with detail kept, cream highlights not white, a whisper of grain.

The specific risk in this register is the flame. Blown-out orange glow is the single thing that drops a live-fire shot into cheap-takeaway territory. Keep the fire, the smoke, the black char edges. Do not let the flame set the white balance. Warm and controlled, never orange and blown.

No orange-teal, no HDR, no heavy vignette, no filter, no cold or blue light.

## Palette, unchanged

Cream, forest, ink, warm copper and amber accents. Copper earns its place more easily in this register than the last. No pure white, no red or orange beyond copper, no gradients, no emerald, mint or purple.

Method is unchanged and still correct: generate two new masters, then hold every subsequent frame image-to-image against them so the set reads as one restaurant.

## New anchor briefs

**Food master.** The signature plate: fire-grilled lamb, suya spice, burnt lime. Shot 50mm equivalent, f2.8 to f4, 30 to 45 degrees above, the honest angle you eat at. Warm ceramic or board. Char proud and dark at the edges, deep red-brown spice crust, real steam, burnt lime cut-side up, a cool herb dressing cutting through. Warm side light. Compose loose with clean space on one side for the animated line and type.

**Interior master.** The room in the new register. 35mm equivalent, f4 to f5.6, eye level from a corner so tables lead the eye in. Warm wood or plaster, simple glassware, warm afternoon light, lived-in and quiet before service. Grill visible or implied if it can be done without reading as a kitchen shot.

## The safe lane, unchanged

AI may generate: food, plated dishes, bread, the room, laid tables, still-life, textures, produce, steam, linen, the hook and line as an object. An unidentifiable hand pouring or turning is acceptable.

AI may never generate: people meant to be real trainees, identifiable faces, or hands sold as the documentary truth of a named person. That line is safeguarding, not aesthetics, and it does not move with the register.

## Real shoot only, unchanged (Doc 16)

The pass and hands, Anne's portrait, the team, willing graduates, the training journey.

Added in this register, and now core rather than supporting: **a trainee at the grill.** Tongs in hand, smoke rising, 35mm at f4, eye level, warm and unstaged. The reference image Anne brought has no person in it, and a person is what separates this brand from every other fire-grill restaurant. This frame carries the social mission.

Consent first, in writing, freely given. Where consent is not given, tell it in the hands, the smoke, the back, the work. Never a generated stand-in.

## The two known AI tells to design out, unchanged

1. Readable text. Menus, labels, handwriting all come out as gibberish cursive. Keep text out of frame.
2. Garnish artefacts. No tapioca pearls, no foam. In this register these are off-brief anyway.

Watch hands and fingers on any pour, and any bottle label.

Negatives to keep on every prompt: no plastic sheen, no HDR, no cold or blue light, no deformed fingers, no clutter, no text.

## Standing method

Taste pass runs on the pixels attached back into chat, not on trust. If a frame drifts from the anchors, pull the anchor harder and reroll that one frame only.
