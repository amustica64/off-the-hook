## 3. The live header, read from source

Now that the artwork is in hand I read it directly rather than inferring from
the rendered page. The audit published earlier today holds, and one claim in it
is now confirmed from the file rather than from a description:

```
logo-primary-light.svg   13 paths at #3E5E3A, one rect at #FAF5E9
logo-primary-dark.svg    13 paths at #8FB086, one rect at #161210
<text> elements          0
```

`#3E5E3A` is `--forest-600`, `#FAF5E9` is `--cream-50`. Doc 08 §4.3 line 109:
"The logo is never used in `--forest-600` on `--cream-50` as the primary
combination. Forest is the accent, not the wordmark colour. Ink stays ink." The
breach is confirmed at source. The dark variant also puts the whole wordmark in
a green where §4.3 asks for `--cream-25`.

In fairness to the artwork: zero `<text>` elements means it is fully outlined
and cannot be re-typeset, which is exactly what §4.4 asks for.

The deployed `components/site/logo.tsx` puts both SVGs in the DOM and hides one
with CSS on `html[data-theme]`, to avoid a flash and a hydration mismatch. That
is a sound approach and worth keeping whichever mark wins.
