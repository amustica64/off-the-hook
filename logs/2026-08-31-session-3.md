## 5. The badge breach, fixed

Doc 08 §4.4: the logo is never "placed inside a container, box, or badge unless
it is the reverse variant used on a dark photo". `mark-badge.png` is the
monogram inside a filled circle, and it sat on cream. `mark-o.png` is the same
artwork unbadged and was already in the repository, unused.

Swapped in `components/site/logo.tsx`. Because header and footer share the
component, it corrects both.

**It was not the one-line change it looks like.** The badge is square at
210x210 and the monogram is 200x230. Keeping the old `width={30} height={30}`
would have condensed it by 13 percent, and §4.4 forbids stretching and
condensing in the same list that forbids badging. Fixing one breach by opening
another is not a fix. The intrinsic 200x230 is now passed through with the
height set in CSS, so the ratio stays exact, and `rounded-full` is gone since
it existed only to crop the badge.

Verified on a production build: eight references to `mark-o.png`, zero to
`mark-badge.png`, and no `rounded-full` left in the component. Lint clean.

`mark-badge.png` is left in place. It is the correct asset for the reverse
variant on a dark photo that §4.4 carves out, and deleting it is a brand call.
